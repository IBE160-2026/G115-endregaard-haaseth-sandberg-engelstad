1 Optimism: Budzier (Oxford Global Projects) slides "Challenges, Causes, and Cures of Major Projects", Project Risk Map: Buildings cost overrun 62%, benefits -5%, frequency 7 of 10, schedule overrun 32%, cost black swans 20% (column order verified vs Olympics row: schedule 0%). https://maxpert.de/lp_mi24dl/files/Alexander_Budzier_-_Challenges,_Causes,_Cures.pdf (date unknown, ~2024). RCF 3 steps; 16,000+ projects dataset (podcast/secondary). Valle Wood: planned 2.5 wk/floor, took longer (TU 2018).
2 Wind: CPA TCIG TIN 020 (UK, 2019, via snippet): manual max typically 20 m/s (72 km/h); UK industry recommended out-of-service 16.5 m/s (60 km/h) — wait snippet said 38 mph 16.5 m/s; operator may stop lower; not overridden. Valle Wood: crane had to hold beams until bolted (TU 2018) -> crane-time sensitivity.
3 Strike: Finnish political strikes Feb–Apr 2024; ports closed; ended 8 Apr 2024 after four weeks; congestion persisted (Kuehne+Nagel 2024-03-28 update); birch plywood availability hit (EUWID 2024-04-04).
4 Archaeology: Oslo kommune "Alle gravearbeider i middelalderbyen krever arkeologisk overvåkning, og noen ganger også utgraving" https://www.oslo.kommune.no/slik-bygger-vi-oslo/middelalderbyen/ ; kulturminneloven § 9 investigation duty on developer, "kostbare, byrdefulle og tidkrevende" (regjeringen.no Oslo arkeologiprosjektet); Bjørvika B4 ballast site fieldwork 4 Jan–22 Jun 2017, developer Stor-Oslo Eiendom (Norsk Maritimt Museum report, snippet); Follobanen NIKU 2013–2018 largest in Oslo medieval town in 100+ yrs; Klypen 22.4.2014–14.4.2015; state 31 MNOK for Klemenskirken.
5 Contaminated ground: Oslo PBE: causes include general city-centre activity; tilstandsklasse 2+ = contaminated, must go to approved landfill; tiltaksplan (forurensningsforskriften kap. 2); Bispevika B6b conditions. Cost: Fornebu (Bærum) extra 6–7 MNOK for sending contaminated masses to external landfill (TU, date unknown).
6 Prices: SSB 2021-06-11: building cost index dwellings +5.8% y/y May 2021; materials +9.5%; timber detached houses materials +11.8%; "store prisøkningene på trelast som har pågått siden høsten 2020". NVE report 2021 (snippet): iron & steel index +40%+, reinforcement and structural steel ~43–44%, concrete 9.9%. EBA 2022-04-06: timber and steel prices "eksploderte i 2020"; steel up sharply (Russia/Ukraine, transport, energy); delivery problems; recommends index regulation.
7 Bankruptcy: TU/NTB/SSB 2024-08-14: Q2 2024 29% of 1,085 company bankruptcies in construction. Case: Nye Kirkenes sykehus (TU 2017-12-21): groundworks firm Kirkenes byggeservice bankrupt; overall ~8 months delay (multiple causes).
8 Change requests: NTNU master 2023 "Kostnadsutvikling og kostnadsestimering i byggeprosjekter": avg change cost 13.9% of actual contract cost, 64.1% client-initiated; 10 cases. NS 8407 31.1: client cannot impose changes beyond 15% net addition (CMS 2025). ASEE: 5–10% change-order growth expected.
9 Frost: manufacturer datasheet: curing stops below +5 °C; concrete must not freeze before ~5 MPa; winter measures: insulation, covers, heating. No Norwegian cost/time figure found.
10 CLT moisture: TU debate 2023-10-12 (SINTEF/NTNU): ZEB lab ~2,000 m2 CLT, heavy rain before roof; no element covered before <15% moisture by weight documented; air dryers; no damage. Danish Byggeskadefonden (TU 2019-09-24): cracking, mould, moisture uptake; recommends covering CLT during construction. SINTEF 2018: drying started after 4 months in compact-roof test; "ved tvil: etabler fuktovervåking".
Høyblokka: TU 2021-06-27: excavation (from Jan 2021) revealed weathered rock unable to carry the block; new point foundations; no delay/cost stated.

# v0.5 walkable site

## 2026-10-27 kl. 20:47 — Trond Engelstad

### Oppgave
Rebuild the game so the player (Statsbygg) walks the site with the project manager and the site manager to foremen who wait with a «!», and solves each challenge in a zoomed-in meeting of the four round a 3D model of the problem. Also asked for: named people (Elisabeth, Hedda, Oskar, «Formann VVS» and so on), a funny HMS check, a busier left menu, a review of the disciplines' estimated hours at the start, more site detail (crane, workers), a better story and better 3D. Kai (game dev) and Vera (3D) were to do it together.

### Plan
- Engine untouched (pure and deterministic). Story, walking and scenes are presentation only, driven from `main.ts`.
- Pure, tested modules: `src/ui/scene/walk.ts` (collision, routes round the pit, foremen's spots, meeting layout) and `src/ui/story.ts` (objectives, meeting lines, HMS checks, kickoff, staffing reviews).
- 3D: `scene/cast.ts` (articulated people), `scene/challenge.ts` (one model per event), `scene/props.ts` (site detail), and `view3d.ts` (avatar, followers, markers, scenes, cinematic camera, articulated instanced workers, bloom and environment lighting).
- HTML: `ui/scenes.ts` (kickoff, review, HMS, talk card, objective, team) and the meeting and decision panel in `ui/play.ts`; the rail in `ui/hud.ts`.

### Resultat
- v0.5.0. The intro runs: the player is at the gate, Oskar stops them (missing safety glasses, with three replies, two of which earn a «Tilsnakk!»), and the start-up meeting at the site offices reviews every discipline (estimate, foreman's pitch, crew and mode controls, Elisabeth's proposal, the forecast against both targets). After that the clock runs.
- Challenges: the foreman of the affected task gets a «!» (colour by kind), a ring and an arrow show the way, and G or «Gå dit» walks there. On arrival the camera cuts to the meeting: lines from the raiser, the dilemma, then the foreman, Hedda and Elisabeth one by one; the decision shows each proposal on the option it backs. The fork card closes the meeting.
- Each new chapter opens a staffing review (Hedda). Oskar's checks after chapters 1, 3 and 5 add ear defenders, a chin strap and gloves to the avatar.
- The rail shows the objective, the team (with status) and a hint and badge per panel. E talks to whoever stands next to the player; clicking a person walks there.
- Names: `Person.name`, `Task.trade`. Oskar (`hms`) added with a generated icon (`people/hms.svg`).
- 3D: the tower crane stands from E, plus a mobile crane, a concrete pump, scaffolding that follows F and G, a material yard, toilets, skips, floodlights, a pit railing and a signboard. Workers have swinging legs and arms, hammer when working, and number one per three workers (max 160), plus yard, hut and gate crews.
- Demo links: `?scene=hms`, `?scene=kickoff&steg=9`, `?uke=60&hendelse=change-security&steg=9`.
- `npm test`: 184 passed (new: walk.test.ts, story.test.ts). `npm run build`: OK (774 kB JS). Verified with headless Chrome screenshots (software WebGL): intro, HMS check, kickoff plan, meeting and decision.
- Not committed (the group decides when to commit and push; the changelog, dilemmas and README are updated).
